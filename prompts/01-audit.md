# Stage 1 — Design Audit

Verify the active design snapshots in `SOURCE-BASELINE.md`, then inspect the complete agreed scope using the matching source adapter.

Create or update `DESIGN-AUDIT.md` from `templates/DESIGN-AUDIT.template.md`.

Inventory screens, flows, viewports, components, variants, styles, variables, assets, content patterns, interactions, states, responsive transformations, accessibility implications, inconsistencies, and missing evidence.

Use:

- `EVD-*` for evidence;
- `AUD-*` for findings;
- precise `SRC-DS-*` and source-region references.

Do not convert observations into product requirements or implementation decisions. Keep Observed, Inferred, Recommended, Confirmed, and Open question distinct.

Perform two reviews:

1. completeness and correctness of the inspected scope;
2. consistency, traceability, source integrity, risks, and uncertainty.

Update `WORKFLOW-STATE.md` and end with files changed, important findings, assumptions, blockers, readiness for requirements, and next permitted action.
