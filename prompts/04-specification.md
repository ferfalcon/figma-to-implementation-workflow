# Stage 4 — Specification

Verify relevant source snapshots, then analyze `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `DESIGN.md`, `DESIGN-AUDIT.md`, and authoritative product or technical documentation.

For Standard and Full, create or update `SPEC.md` from `templates/SPEC.template.md` and follow `guidelines/SPEC.md`.

For Lite, complete the specification section of `IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF.md`.

Translate approved intent into precise, observable, testable behavior covering functionality, content, interactions, keyboard and focus behavior, states, responsive behavior, accessibility, data, validation, errors, recovery, and edge cases.

Use `SPEC-BEH-*`, `SPEC-INT-*`, `SPEC-VAL-*`, `SPEC-ACC-*`, `SPEC-DATA-*`, and `AC-*`. Reference `REQ-*`, `DES-*`, `EVD-*`, and `SRC-*`.

Do not prescribe repository paths or unsupported architecture. Do not invent arbitrary breakpoints, thresholds, or focus rules.

Perform two reviews, update `WORKFLOW-STATE.md`, and report coverage, assumptions, blockers, readiness for documentation review, and next permitted action.
